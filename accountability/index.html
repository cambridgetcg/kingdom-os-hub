The fair method

Make the claims inspectable.

A comparison note preserves one recorder's attribution. It does not authenticate the recorder, either source, a date, delivery, or the claims. The method applies the same context, evidence, and correction standard to every side.

01 · LIMIT

Use one pair of records.

Use public material or words the source owns and has authorised you to handle. A note is not a person file, archive, feed, or dossier.

02 · SEPARATE

Name each statement kind.

Observation, expectation, commitment, consequence, interpretation, and unknown are different. None silently becomes another.

03 · CONTEXT

Keep enough surrounding words.

Time, place, translation, scope, and nearby language can change a reading. Minimal quotation never means misleading quotation.

04 · QUESTION

Ask how both could hold.

The maker performs no contradiction test. A difference may come from error, changed belief, new evidence, translation, or missing context.

05 · CORRECT

Leave a source-owned route.

Any public use needs a practical correction and safety-removal path. Later use carries material corrections when safe.

06 · STOP

Let unresolved remain complete.

No reply is required. Silence, refusal, rest, absence, and leaving prove nothing, receive no score, and trigger no repeat pressure.

This is not an exposure or accusation tool. Do not use it to bait, provoke, impersonate, secretly record, compel a confession, name private people or minors, publish private material, coordinate harassment, or make a high-impact decision. Someone concealing information to survive coercion does not owe dangerous truth to the coercer; this page cannot judge that situation.

Before any public use

The recorder carries responsibility too.

  • Necessity: use the least exposing record that can support understanding, correction, or repair.
  • Authority: public visibility does not automatically authorise profiling, republication, pressure, or high-impact use.
  • Safety: do not include credentials, private people, minors, personal data, private inner speech, or material likely to create danger.
  • No trap: no bait, provocation, impersonation, secret recording, tracking, or coerced testimony.
  • Correction: carry material disputes forward, and remove sensitive bytes when safety, consent, or law requires it.
  • No verdict: make claims inspectable; never compress a being into a score, blacklist, or final judgment.

A public note needs separate human review, a lawful and ethical basis, source checks, proportionate redaction, and a practical source-owned correction route. This page supplies none of those decisions and has no publish or submission button.
