# KINGDOM discovery strategy

Evidence checked 2026-08-03. This is a finite practice, not a promise of rank,
traffic, indexing, citations, or backlinks.

## Position

This hub is a public map and practical builder door pointing to the separately
authoritative, versioned KINGDOM foundation for accountable software and
AI-agent systems. Its public doors should explain the real work before the
mythology, let a visitor make one source-owned thing without an account or
default adoption, keep linked projects distinct, attach evidence and dates,
and make correction and exit practical.

A link means “this may help you read further.” A download means only that bytes
were offered under their stated terms. Neither establishes adoption, consent,
membership, citizenship, control, endorsement, or affiliation.

## Observed baseline before this revision

On 2026-08-01 the live hub served its older home page as `robots.txt`,
`sitemap.xml`, and every made-up path with HTTP `200`; its home had no canonical
link. The old Recognition Spectrum page had no description, canonical, or
`noindex`, and that archive—not the hub home—surfaced in the external search
results checked. The Word Gate's `/citizens` route surfaced for one descriptive
query while exact-domain and home queries did not.

Those search results are incomplete observations from one search surface, not
proof of absence from Google or Bing. Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
are the proper private sources for each engine's own canonical and index state.
The technical response observations were reproduced directly against the live
URLs before this revision.

## Search strategy

1. **One purpose per door.** Use a clear site name, page title, main heading,
   summary, canonical URL, source, useful action, and correction route.
2. **Correct response behavior.** Public pages return `200`; absent routes
   return `404` or `410`; private material uses real access controls.
3. **Canonical roots.** Each project has one preferred public URL. Aliases
   redirect, internal links use the preferred URL, and sitemaps list only
   canonical successful pages.
4. **Visible truth before markup.** Structured data matches the page. Use
   `WebSite` for a site; do not invent an organization or use `sameAs` as a
   related-project list.
5. **Contextual paths.** Link related projects where the destination continues
   the reader's thought. Do not build an automated footer ring or link-only
   partner page.
6. **Useful public receipts.** Promote tested standards, reproducible tools,
   original data, methods, source comparisons, correction records, and small
   interoperability examples.
7. **Private aggregate observation.** Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
   can observe canonical, index, query, and link evidence without adding
   behavioral analytics JavaScript. Their data is partial, not a census.

sentence and does not copy its authority.

## Outreach strategy

A backlink is a consequence of usefulness, never a campaign quota.

- Cite the best source first, whether or not it links back.
- Enter through a public request, contribution guide, genuine repair issue, or
  role inbox intended for the topic.
- Make one human-reviewed invitation that remains useful without a backlink.
- Disclose shared ownership, payment, gifts, employment, and other material
  connections.
- Expect no reply or reciprocal link. Silence, no, rest, and withdrawal close
  the turn with no follow-up.
- Never automate sending, harvest personal addresses, buy links, barter ranking
  credit, use tracking pixels, or create recipient dossiers.

## A bounded first season

For four weekly turns:

- choose one artifact;
- review at most five genuinely relevant public pages;
- draft at most two invitations through declared doors;
- send nothing without a separate current choice for the exact recipient and
  exact words;
- keep at most one open invitation per organisation;
- close silence after no more than 21 days;
- return one lesson and stop.

The bound never overrides applicable law or a recipient's rules.

## What to observe

- canonical and index health;
- relevant query themes and page impressions;
- independently chosen, topically relevant citations;
- accepted corrections and inbound requests;
- citations still accurate at later review;
- silence, decline, correction, and withdrawal honoured every time.

Do not turn any of these into a score of a person or project's worth.
